How it works

Donors list, recipients request, and the donor approves.

  1. 1

    List what's left over

    Add the food, how much there is, where to collect it and the time it must be picked up by.

  2. 2

    Recipients request a share

    People and NGOs browse listings and ask for the amount they need. That amount is held for them.

  3. 3

    Approve and hand over

    The donor approves a request, both sides get each other's contact details, and the food is marked collected.
